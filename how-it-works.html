Clean-Up Notice System

A fast, mobile-first tool for improving site cleanliness and safety — in just a few taps.

📸 Snap It

On-site staff can use their phone to photograph hazards, waste, or unclean areas directly in the form — no app install needed.

✍️ Annotate & Sign

Quickly highlight problem areas with the built-in drawing tool and sign the form for immediate accountability.

📤 Submit & Notify

Once submitted, a professional PDF is automatically generated and emailed to selected recipients like Site Managers or Safety Officers.

📥 View & Track

All submitted notices are logged and viewable in the secure dashboard — track resolutions, monitor deadlines, and close the loop.

⚡ Why it works

It reduces paperwork, speeds up resolution, and encourages cleaner sites by making reporting fast, visual, and accountable.

🔒 Simple & Secure

All data is securely stored. Users log in, submit forms, and only authorised personnel can view or manage notices.

📱 Mobile-First Design

Designed for real-world site use — everything fits on mobile screens and works even with gloves or in bright daylight.
